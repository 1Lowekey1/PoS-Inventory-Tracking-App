## Long version

**Introducing PopPOS: a free, offline cashier and inventory app for pop-ups and small cafés**

If you've ever run a stall alone (making drinks, taking orders, counting change and wondering at the end of the day whether you actually made money), PopPOS was built for you.

PopPOS started as a simple booth cashier for our own coffee pop-ups. Version 3 is a full rebuild, shaped by what actually happens behind a counter:

**Sell fast with one pair of hands**
- Tap products, tap **Charge**, done. Exact cash takes two taps.
- Big buttons, a change calculator for cash, and GCash/Maya/card.
- Group orders made simple: "5 Americanos, 3 with syrup" in one step.
- A *To make* queue, so you never lose track of who's waiting.

**Know your stock without counting cups**
- Every sale deducts its recipe automatically: beans, milk, cups, syrup.
- Live "N left" warnings and "about 1.5 hours left at today's pace".
- Restock what you bought *or made* mid-event, and the costs follow.

**Know if you actually made money**
- Profit the way pop-ups really work: **sales − (capital + expenses)**.
- Break-even progress while you sell.
- Day-by-day and event reports: busiest hours, best sellers, payment split, cash over/short, ingredient usage.

**Works anywhere**
- **No internet needed** after the first open. Install it like an app on your phone, tablet or laptop.
- **No account, no subscription.** Your data stays on your device.
- Optional **receipt printer and cash drawer** support.
