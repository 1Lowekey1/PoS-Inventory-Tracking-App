# Social Media Kit

Ready-to-post captions, plus image and video ideas. Replace `[LINK]` and use **your own** screenshots and clips.

## Launch week (5 posts)

**Day 1: Launch**
> Running a pop-up alone? Taking orders, making drinks, counting change… 😮‍💨
> I built **PopPOS**, a free cashier + inventory app that works **offline**. Two taps for exact cash, recipes that count your stock, and real profit at the end of the day.
> Try the sample menu → [LINK]

*Visual:* phone on a stand next to a cup, Sell screen open.

**Day 2: Group orders**
> "5 Americanos, 3 with syrup, 1 with an extra shot." 😅
> PopPOS: tap the line → *Apply to 3 of 5 cups* → done. The receipt still shows it neatly grouped.

*Visual:* screen recording of the line editor counter.

**Day 3: Stock**
> Stop guessing how many cups you have left. Every sale deducts its recipe: beans, milk, cups, syrup. PopPOS warns you *before* you run out.

*Visual:* Stock tab with a "Low" badge and "~1 h left at today's pace".

**Day 4: Profit**
> Did the bazaar actually make money? 🤔
> PopPOS uses the way pop-ups really buy stock: **sales − (capital + expenses)**, with a break-even bar that fills up as you sell.

*Visual:* Event tab with the break-even meter, numbers blurred if you prefer.

**Day 5: Offline**
> No signal at the venue? PopPOS works completely **offline** and your data never leaves your phone. No account, no subscription.

*Visual:* phone in airplane mode, still selling.
