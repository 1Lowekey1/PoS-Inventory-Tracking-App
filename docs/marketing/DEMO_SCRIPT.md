# Demo Script

Two versions: a **2-minute recorded video** and a **live demo** at a booth or meet-up. Both use the built-in sample menu, so nothing real is affected.

## Prepare (5 minutes before)

1. Open PopPOS on the demo device (preferably a fresh browser profile or a spare device).
2. Tap **Try a sample coffee menu**.
3. Event → **Start event**: "Demo Market", 1 day, capital ₱1,500, planned output 50, float ₱500.
4. Settings: Tile size **Large**, theme to suit the room.
5. Optional: connect a receipt printer and test print.

To reset afterwards: Settings → Danger zone → **Erase everything** (type ERASE), on the demo device only.

## Live demo (3–5 minutes)

**Open with their problem**, not the app: *"How do you keep track of cups and change when you're alone at the stall?"*

1. **Let them sell.** Hand them the device: "Make an order for me: two lattes." Most people get it without help.
2. **Group order.** "Now five Americanos, three with syrup." Show *Apply to 3 of 5*.
3. **Mistake.** "Oops, wrong one." Tap **Undo**.
4. **Stock.** Show the Stock tab: "That's what you just used."
5. **Money.** Event tab: break-even. "This is the number you actually care about."
6. **Offline.** Turn on airplane mode and keep selling.
7. **Close.** "It's free. Here's the link / QR."

**Common questions** (answers are in the [FAQ](../customer/FAQ.md)): Is it free? Does it need internet? Can it print? Official receipts? Two phones at once? (Not yet: one device per event.)

**Don't** demo on a device holding a real event's data.
