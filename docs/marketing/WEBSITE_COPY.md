## Three reasons (feature strip)

**⚡ Sell fast, alone.** Two taps for exact cash. Change calculator, GCash/Maya, and group orders in one step.

**📦 Stock that counts itself.** Recipes deduct every ingredient as you sell, with warnings before you run out.

**💰 Know if you made money.** Sales − (capital + expenses), with break-even tracking and day-by-day reports.

## How it works {#how}

1. **Set up your menu once.** Add ingredients and products with their recipes. Or start from the sample coffee menu.
2. **Start an event.** Enter what you spent up front and open Day 1 with your cash float.
3. **Sell.** Tap, charge, done. The queue shows who's waiting; stock updates itself.
4. **Close the day.** Count the cash, see over/short, print a summary, back up.
5. **See the results.** Busiest hours, best sellers, ingredient usage, and profit for the event.

## FAQ (short)

- **Is it free?** Yes, with no subscription or account.
- **Does it need internet?** Only to open it the first time.
- **Does it print receipts?** Yes, on ESC/POS thermal printers (USB/Bluetooth), or through any printer via the print dialog.
- **Are the receipts official?** No. PopPOS isn't BIR-accredited.
- **Coming from Booth POS?** Your products, stock and past sales can be imported.
